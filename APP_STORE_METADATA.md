## Description

Sort colors, solve puzzles, and bring a little island back to life.

Move pieces between vessels to gather four of the same color together. Each
piece has a symbol as well as a color. Take your time, use a free hint when
you’re stuck, or undo a move and try a different route.

• Explore 100 puzzles across five difficulty tiers.
• Earn coins and stars, unlock worlds, and replay levels to improve your score.
• Restore ten island features, including a flower garden, bridge, and lighthouse.
• Pick up an unfinished puzzle after closing the app.
• Play offline with no timer.

Every puzzle and the full island can be completed through play.
